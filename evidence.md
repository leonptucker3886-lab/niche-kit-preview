## Evidence

### 1. Private well water testing
- Private wells are not EPA-regulated; testing falls on owners → education gap, little advertising.
- Tap Score runs an active affiliate program through AWIN: https://mytapscore.com/pages/become-a-tap-score-affiliate
- WQA partners with Tap Score for local treatment referrals (second revenue layer): https://wqa.org/tap-score/
- New England bedrock geology raises arsenic/uranium/radon risk (verify county data with NH DES / Maine CDC before launch).

### 2. Radon — Iowa
- EPA Map of Radon Zones: all Iowa counties are Zone 1 (highest potential): https://www.epa.gov/radon/epa-map-radon-zones
- EPA estimates ~1 in 15 U.S. homes is at/above the 4 pCi/L action level: https://www.findradonpros.com/guides/radon-statistics
- Monetize kits (Tap Score radon water test: https://mytapscore.com/products/radon-water-test) plus mitigation leads via home-services CPL/pay-per-call networks: https://vellko.com/blog/home-services-affiliate-programs/

### 3. Septic — rural Vermont
- Practitioner account from a real ad account: "septic in particular is a very noncompetitive niche": https://adaptdigitalsolutions.com/articles/google-ads-for-septic-companies/
- Pay-per-call networks carry household-services offers nationwide: https://diggitymarketing.com/best-affiliate-programs/pay-per-call/
- Cinch home warranty sells septic and well-pump add-ons (affiliate listed): https://ucompares.com/affiliate/home-services/

## Why these correlate
1. **Low ad competition:** dull, rural, need-based services; few national brands bidding locally.
2. **High intent:** health risk (radon, arsenic) or urgent failure (septic) → strong conversion on call CTAs.
3. **Active payouts:** each niche has at least one live affiliate, CPL or pay-per-call program.

## Launch checklist (turnkey)
1. Apply to AWIN (Tap Score) and one pay-per-call network; swap `AFFILIATE_LINK_HERE`, `TRACKING_NUMBER_HERE`, `FORM_ENDPOINT_HERE`.
2. Deploy each HTML file as-is (Vercel/Netlify/any static host).
3. Add a county-specific page per town; claim Google Business Profile only if you partner with a real local provider.
4. Keep the commission disclosure in the footer (FTC).

## Caveats
- Commission rates vary per offer and weren't published uniformly; confirm terms on approval.
- Ad-competition claims are from industry sources, not your own keyword-planner pulls — run Google Keyword Planner for each target ZIP before spending.
